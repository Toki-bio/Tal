SINEderella · satellite screen

SINE satellite loci

Tandem arrays that carry SINE sequence, as found by the satellite stage before the SINE analysis: loci whose monomer is a part of the SINE (kind A, verified by TRF and alignment) and arrays of regular spacing whose unit is longer than the SINE (kind B). Every number here can be checked at the level of one locus.

Data

Paths are relative to the Tal repository (raw GitHub) or full URLs. Examples: cobra Squam3C · gecko Squam3A · rle (bats) · tbr

Summary

Where the monomers sit on the SINE

For each consensus: how many kind-A loci cover each position of the SINE (from the aligned part of the monomer). A satellite derived from one part of the SINE shows as one block; the paper's sSat3 of snakes covers Squam3 positions 42–108.

Monomers per locus

Kind-A loci by monomer count. Satellites that have amplified show a tail of long loci; "nuclei" of 4–9 monomers dominate everywhere.

Locus table

Click a column to sort. Coordinates are 0-based BED starts; copy them into a genome browser or samtools faidx.
