Chiroptera — bat SINEs

Rhin-1 and VES across bat families, one genome per family, grouped by clade. ← Tal SINE main page

Overview

Clades and family order follow the time-calibrated family tree of Hao et al. 2023 (Integrative Zoology 19:989–998, Fig. 3). Every genome was searched with SINEbase Rhin-1 and VES as the only consensuses (IUPAC codes resolved to a base, so both keep full length); the earlier Rhinolophus runs used Rhin-1 alone. Copies = firm + soft assigned (firm count below); sim = median copy bitscore / consensus self-bitscore. Prior evidence is the SINE label on the annotated copy of that figure. De novo = AnnoSINE2 + SINEbase-fragment scan candidates, clustered by SubFam for manual review (run for hla and tbr first). 9 of 23 genomes published.
