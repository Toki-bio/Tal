Family tree — Rhin-1 and VES per family

Topology, node ages and colours redrawn from Hao et al. 2023, Fig. 3 (MrBayes time tree; triangles = collapsed families from their crown age). Each leaf shows the copies found in that family's genome(s): Rhin = Rhin-1, VES = VES; solid = 1,000 copies or more, pale = 1–999, NA = not searched, … = search running. Where a family has several genomes the largest count is shown (hover for all). Species names link to their reports.
