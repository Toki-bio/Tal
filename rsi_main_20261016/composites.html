rsi composites: parts and shared sequence

Bars to scale. Coloured blocks = declared parts at their placed positions on the composite (one colour per unit); grey = linker or sequence that is no declared unit. Thin tracks under a bar = stretches similar in sequence (ssearch36, ≥ 30 bp, ≥ 80 %) to a differently named unit, one track per such unit, drawn in that unit's colour (best 4 units; label = unit and best identity; hover a stripe for coordinates). A unit and its own copy inside a composite are not counted.
