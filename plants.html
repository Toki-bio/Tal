Workflow

  1. AnnoSINE_v2 2.0.9, mode 3 (structure and homology search), plant profiles, whole genome, RepeatMasker step off.
  2. Comparison set from Repbase (the repeat library of the Genetic Information Research Institute): 84 land-plant SINE entries, 83 of 95–369 bp plus apple SINE-3_Mad (797 bp), used for the match table above. Repbase sequences are licensed and are not published here.
  3. Peeling of the seed plate into groups, one consensus per group.
  4. SINEderella (commit b5c8a4a): search, copy assignment, flank test, alignments and report, with 8 queries: the 7 peeled consensuses and Repbase SINE-3_Mad. Repbase SINE-1_Mad was removed in favour of the nearly identical consensus_354seqs, and the copies were assigned again.
