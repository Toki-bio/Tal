Workflow

  1. AnnoSINE_v2 2.0.9, mode 3 (structure and homology search), plant profiles, whole genome, RepeatMasker step off.
  2. Query set from Repbase (the repeat library of the Genetic Information Research Institute): 84 land-plant SINE entries, 83 of 95–369 bp plus apple SINE-3_Mad (797 bp). Repbase sequences are licensed and are not published here.
  3. SINEderella (commit b5c8a4a): search, copy assignment, flank test and report, with the Repbase entries and the AnnoSINE_v2 seeds as queries.
