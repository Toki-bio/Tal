Sicista trizona gene gaps, gene by gene

One card per gene: alignment of both trizona and both betulina alleles, HiFi reads in ViewAlign, an IGV session, and the mobile elements in the indels. Unpublished, for review. ← Sicista SINE page

TMEM114 heterozygous deletion

Deposited hap1 has lost a 63,566 bp segment that holds the whole gene (all 4 exons). The segment is intact on deposited hap2 at OZ418090.1:110,290,353-110,353,918, and both betulina haplotypes carry it, so this is a deletion on trizona hap1, not an insertion on hap2. The hap1 junction is at OZ418350.1:111,028,553 (±1 bp, one shared base at the break). There is no N within 300 kb of the locus on either haplotype, so nothing here can be repaired: the sequence is absent from that allele. 14 HiFi reads cross the hap1 junction by at least 1 kb on both sides, and 8 and 9 cross the two hap2 breakpoints.

Sequences in the alignment: hap1 OZ418350.1:111,012,846-111,045,261, hap2 OZ418090.1:110,274,645-110,370,690, betulina h1tg000312l:6,327,125-6,423,689 and h2tg001095l:629,999-722,000 (reverse strand); each matches its assembly exactly. Coordinates in final.tsv are in the repaired assemblies (+254,422 bp on hap1, +709,928 bp on hap2 here), not the deposited ones.

Mobile elements in the TMEM114 indels

Indels of 50 bp or more were taken from direct pairwise alignments of the four sequences, cut out, and searched with ssearch36 (E ≤ 1e-5) against the local RepBase_filtered bank (12,030 sequences), SINEBase and the five Dip consensuses. Full table with positions: TMEM114_indel_TEs.tsv.

Only 26 % of the deleted segment matches the library. Its elements are old fragments, and none spans the deletion, so no single element explains it. Matches to Hydra, Perkinsus, salmon, Aplysia and zebrafish elements are not credible classifications. Between species the clearest events are full-length DIP0 SINEs (1-222 of 222) in betulina that trizona lacks. Betulina hap1 alone also carries a 5,065 bp L1 (L1_Mur3 ORF2, 74 %) and a DIP0 SINE that betulina hap2 lacks, so these are polymorphic within betulina.
