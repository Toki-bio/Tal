Sicista trizona gene gaps, gene by gene

One card per gene: alignment of both trizona and both betulina alleles, HiFi reads in ViewAlign, an IGV session, and the mobile elements in the indels. Unpublished, for review. ← Sicista SINE page · Final gap recovery

TMED4 gene on both haplotypes

The hap1 N run OZ418347.1:96,007,475-96,007,574 sits inside the gene and hides 4 of its 5 exons. Hap2 has all 5. The gene-search version fills the N with 2,331 bp copied from hap2 (OZ418087.1:95,802,105-95,804,435), which restores 5 of 5 exons. That fill is the hap2 allele, not hap1's own sequence.

One read shows what hap1 really has: ERR16751478.985790 matches the hap1 flanks (about 11 differences, against 260 on hap2) and crosses the gap. It carries all 5 exons, so the gene is on hap1 too. Between the flanks it has about 2,500 bp, about 170 bp more than the hap2 fill, with 20 substitutions and 7 indels in the aligned part. Hap1 is under-read here: fished again from the whole read file, 18 hap1 and 26 hap2 reads lie near the gap, and only this one hap1 read crosses it. One read is below the 3-read repair rule, so hap1's exact sequence is shown but not accepted as a repair.

EPS8L1 not repaired, hap1 unknown

The hap1 N run OZ418348.1:12,671,658-12,671,757 stands where hap2 has about 16 kb (near OZ418088.1:13,047,934-13,064,230) holding exons 1 to 18 and the end of exon 19. Hap1 keeps only exon 20 and most of exon 19; hap2 has all 20. It stays N: the region was classed as tandem and no read crosses it. All 14 reads near the gap fit hap2 better, so I have no read showing what hap1 has there. The gene is present on hap2, and whether hap1 has it is unknown.

TMEM114 heterozygous deletion

Deposited hap1 has lost a 63,566 bp segment that holds the whole gene (all 4 exons). The segment is intact on deposited hap2 at OZ418090.1:110,290,353-110,353,918, and both betulina haplotypes carry it, so this is a deletion on trizona hap1, not an insertion on hap2. The hap1 junction is at OZ418350.1:111,028,553 (±1 bp, one shared base at the break). There is no N within 300 kb of the locus on either haplotype, so nothing here can be repaired: the sequence is absent from that allele. 14 HiFi reads cross the hap1 junction by at least 1 kb on both sides, and 8 and 9 cross the two hap2 breakpoints.

Sequences in the alignment: hap1 OZ418350.1:111,012,846-111,045,261, hap2 OZ418090.1:110,274,645-110,370,690, betulina h1tg000312l:6,327,125-6,423,689 and h2tg001095l:629,999-722,000 (reverse strand); each matches its assembly exactly. Coordinates in final.tsv are in the repaired assemblies (+254,422 bp on hap1, +709,928 bp on hap2 here), not the deposited ones.

Mobile elements in the TMEM114 indels

Only 26 % of the deleted segment matches the library. Its elements are old fragments, and none spans the deletion, so no single element explains it. Matches to Hydra, Perkinsus, salmon, Aplysia and zebrafish elements are not credible classifications. Between species the clearest events are full-length DIP0 SINEs (1-222 of 222) in betulina that trizona lacks. Betulina hap1 alone also carries a 5,065 bp L1 (L1_Mur3 ORF2, 74 %) and a DIP0 SINE that betulina hap2 lacks, so these are polymorphic within betulina.
