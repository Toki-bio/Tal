Sicista trizona gene gaps, gene by gene

One card per gene: alignment of both trizona and both betulina alleles, HiFi reads in ViewAlign, an IGV session, and the mobile elements in the indels. Unpublished, for review. ← Sicista SINE page

TMEM114 heterozygous deletion

The gene allele sits on deposited hap2 at OZ418090.1:110,274,646-110,370,691 (96 kb, 4 of 4 exons, with a ~63.5 kb segment inside). On deposited hap1 the same place, OZ418350.1:111,012,847-111,045,262, lacks that segment, including the whole gene. The gene is present in the animal on one haplotype. There is no repaired version, because the sequence is absent from the hap1 allele, not hidden in an N gap. Both coordinates were checked by mapping the alignment sequences back (exact full-length matches).

Reads were taken from the HiCanu read-to-contig BAM (contigs tig00001945 for hap1, tig00006196 for hap2) and mapped to the deposited-genome windows, so they are the reads that assembled these alleles. Reads open at the start of the window, not on the gene.

Mobile elements in the TMEM114 indels

Every indel of 50 bp or more in the alignment was cut out and searched with ssearch36 (E ≤ 1e-5) against RepBase (the local RepBase_filtered bank, 12,030 sequences), SINEBase and the five Dip consensuses. Hits were tiled by score. Full table with genomic positions: TMEM114_indel_TEs.tsv.

The ~63.5 kb hap2 segment is a mosaic of old, fragmented repeats (L1 LX8 up to 1.3 kb at 63–76 % identity, CR1, ERV3/MaLR, ERV1, a Gypsy fragment, 11 Dip and 20 other SINE copies at 77–87 %). No single full-length element explains it. The largest trizona-vs-betulina differences are an ERV1 and an L1 (L1_Mur3 ORF2) of 3 kb each.
