The last round ran sineclose lr on every gap of both haplotypes, with all assemblies as fill sources and a search of the whole HiFi file. It found 78 fills that round 2 did not have. One gene changed: NOSIP became readable on hap1. 14 genes at 8 gaps are still hidden on hap1, and hap2 shows every one of them.

Strict holds joins and inserts only. Gene-search also holds one parent's sequence at gaps where the other parent could not be read, so it is for finding genes. Assemblies: therioserver:/home/toki/gof/final/.

How to read the views

Alignment rows: the deposited haplotype with its N run, the final version with the fill (when there is one), the other haplotype, betulina primary, and reads that cross the gap (read_hap1_1 and so on: up to 3 of this haplotype, 1 of the other). The annotation track marks the N run, the fill and, for genes, the exons.

Reads opens ViewAlign in Reads mode on the final window, or on the deposited window where the gap is still N. Every read name starts with its phase: hap1|, hap2|, tie| or nophase|, with _cross added when the read crosses the gap, for example hap1_cross|ERR16751478.240853.

A read is phased by its differences from each haplotype over the same read bases in the flanks. "Crosses" means at least 400 of the 500 bp on each side of the gap are aligned. Reads were fished again from the whole file with words from both haplotypes, so these counts are new and independent of the run that made the fills. A read made only of repeat cannot be fished. Windows reach 5 kb past the gap, 25 kb for genes. Reads have soft clips and qualities removed to keep the files small.

NOSIP gene on both haplotypes

The hap1 N run OZ418348.1:32,408,489-32,408,588 hides 6 of the 8 exons. The gene-search fill of 6,094 bp restores all 8, but it is the hap2 allele. All 5 hap2 reads crossing the gap match it with at most 1 substitution.

One hap1 read shows what hap1 really has. ERR16751478.240853 fits the hap1 flanks (7 differences, against 132 on hap2), crosses the gap and carries all 8 exons, so the gene is on hap1 too. Against the fill it has 39 substitutions, 20 indels and one 246 bp insertion. One read is below the 3-read rule, so its sequence is shown here and not used as a repair. This is the same result as TMED4.

The 78 new fills

16 wrong allele, 15 own allele or identical, 3 undecided, and 44 that cannot be tested. Wrong allele means reads of this haplotype cross the fill and differ from it: the place is right and the sequence belongs to the other parent. Gene-search takes all 78. Strict takes the joins and inserts except the 3 proven wrong alleles.

Repeating the phase test on the fresh reads gave the same verdict for 77 of 78; gap0097 moved from flanks tie to no own read crosses. Both of those mean the fill cannot be tested.
