What these plates are

Each plate shows one subfamily consensus of the run and the human tRNA gene (GtRNAdb hg19, 419 genes) it matches best. They are aligned by the same ssearch36 local alignment that scored the head in SINEderella's tRNA step (tools/trna_heads.sh, plates by tools/trna_plates.py). The aligned stretch is in upper case and the rest in lower case.

Isotype tied: when the best gene of a different amino acid scores within 2 bits of the best, that gene is a third row of the plate and a second line in the table. Tied here: consensus_10seqs_noterm (Ile 43.0 bits, Lys 41.6); consensus_386seqs (Pro 32.0 bits, Ala 31.3); consensus_47seqs (Lys 28.9 bits, Ser 28.6).

Shuffles is the best score of 20 dinucleotide shuffles of the same consensus against the same genes; a head counts when its score is above that. MEG-RL and MEG-RS are 5S rRNA-derived (Rfam 5S_rRNA, positions 1-119), so their best tRNA hit falls below the shuffles. A composite's plate shows its best-scoring head only.
