All mammalian SINEs — SINEderella with the 58-consensus SINEbase bank, S. betulina hifiasm primary

Whole-genome run (2026-10-06/07, therioserver, SINEderella 2fac340) of the 58 SINEbase consensuses classed Mammalia on the primary assembly (1,144 contigs, 2.99 Gb). Before assignment the satellite screen removed 8,134 hits that lie in 1,359 SINE-derived satellite loci and 210 verified tandem arrays; the remaining 2,172,946 copies were assigned by the 10-cycle vote (1,935,999 firm, the rest soft). Plates carry 50 bp left and 70 bp right flanks, the consensus is row 1 and the sequence as searched row 2. SubFam 30k is the SubFam alignment of the random 30,000-copy sample (600 chunk consensi plus the 58 bank consensuses); the per-family SubFam plates exist for families with at least 400 copies. Not yet curated by hand.

Dip SINE — SINEderella on the whole genome

SINEderella steps 1–4 and the report on the whole genome, searched with five consensuses: SINEbase DIP and the literature subfamilies Dip_a1, Dip_a2, Dip_b1, Dip_b2 (as searched: IUPAC codes removed, so DIP is 206 bp, Dip_a1 224, Dip_b1 240). Assignment is by the 10-cycle unanimous vote; firm copies passed it, soft copies did not but have a best-scoring query. The subfamilies share most of their sequence, so most copies are also hit by the others (the leak and conflict columns in the full report). Alignments carry 50 bp left and 70 bp right flanks, the consensus is row 1 and the sequence as searched is row 2; a subfamily with fewer than 100 firm copies has its 100-copy plates filled with soft copies (marked [soft]). No subfamilies have been curated by hand yet.

De novo SINE search

SINE-de-novo-genome-scan (50 bp fragments of SINEbase and literature consensuses, ssearch36, identity ≥65%, query coverage ≥0.90, hits merged within 500 bp) merged with AnnoSINE2 seeds where run, k-mer thinned (singletons dropped), a random 30,000-copy sample, and SubFam chunks of 50 (rows are chunk consensuses ordered by similarity). Two searches per genome:

  • 10% subsample, with AnnoSINE2. A seeded random 300 × 1 Mb windows of each genome (the full-genome scan with Dip present was projected at over 20 h per genome). All AnnoSINE2 seeds are kept in the sample.
  • Dip-depleted whole genome, scan only. Every Dip-bank hit (about 258 Mb in S. betulina, 264 Mb in S. trizona) is cut out first, pieces ≥100 bp are scanned; about 2 h per genome.

Orthologous Dip loci — S. betulina vs S. trizona (SINE_orth_loc)

SINE_orth_loc (GitHub version) on the firmly assigned Dip loci of the two whole-genome runs above (1,032,760 in S. betulina, 1,036,280 in S. trizona; element: Dip_a1). The 300 bp flank on one side of every locus is mapped to the other genome with bwa mem; each orthologous pair is aligned together with the consensus and classified by ComPair. Only loci with a unique, mappable flank enter, so these are counts of validated locus pairs, not of copies. Without an outgroup the direction cannot be read: a locus present in one species only is an insertion in that lineage or a deletion in the other. The Jaculus jaculus genome is the intended outgroup.

Subfamily = the firmly assigned Dip locus that overlaps the aligned element on the side that carries it (S. betulina for the first two classes, S. trizona for the third). “No firm locus”: the alignment shows the element but the earlier assignment did not call it firmly. sim_ratio is the similarity to the assigned consensus (about 1 young, below 0.5 diverged) and exists for only part of the loci (sim_scores.tsv), so the medians are a rough age proxy; the two genomes have different overall values (Dip_a1 median 0.685 in S. betulina, 0.653 in S. trizona), so compare within a genome, not across.
