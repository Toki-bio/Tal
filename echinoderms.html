Workflow

No ophiuroid SINE family is known (see Literature), so the bank is built de novo, from two discovery arms per genome, pooled, then run through SINEderella:

  1. AnnoSINE_v2 mode 3 (structure + homology), -a 1 (animal Dfam HMMs).
  2. SINE-de-novo-genome-scan (sine_scan.sh) with the query bank v2.2: 1801 fragments of 50 bp from SINEbase (231), the post-2015 literature collection (395) and ten RepBase echinoderm SINEs (Patiria miniata Pami, Paracentrotus lividus SINE2-1_PL, Strongylocentrotus purpuratus SINE2-4/4a/4a1/4b/4c/8, SPEC1, SURF1).
  3. Candidates from both arms → SubFam → consensus bank → SINEderella (full run, report per species).

AnnoSINE fix (2026-11-18): genomes whose first sequence starts with a G-free telomeric repeat (ccctaa…) made nhmmer fail to guess the alphabet, so AnnoSINE silently ran without its homology arm (affects O. fragilis, O. longicauda). Fixed by nhmmer --dna plus the matching one-line shift in AnnoSINE's output parser; positive control (30 inserted MIR copies) recovered as one seed.

Literature

No named SINE family from any brittle star was found in the literature, Dfam or RepBase. Echinoderm SINEs with consensus sequences come only from sea urchins and one sea star (above).

  • Amphiura filiformis genome (Parey et al. 2024, Nature Ecology & Evolution): “The repeat family SINE/tRNA-Deu-L2 is significantly associated with breakpoint locations and may have contributed to the Hox1-Hox3 inversion through non-homologous repair”. Their repeat library is in the Zenodo record 10.5281/zenodo.10785182 (checked 2026-11-18: it holds the soft-masked genome but no repeat consensus library, so no ophiuroid SINE consensus is available from it).
  • Ophiura sarsii (Sci Data 2024, Table 8): SINE 1.51% of the genome, combined homology + de novo; no families named.
  • Ophioderma brevispinum (BMC Genomics 2022): “Short interspersed nuclear elements (SINEs) … amounted to 0.11%”, all classed as MIR.
  • Dfam (API, 2026-11-18): 0 families under Ophiuroidea (taxid 7618); 0 SINE families under Echinodermata (7586).
  • Patiria miniata Pami: CORE-domain SINE, Luchetti & Mantovani 2016 (Curr Zool). Paracentrotus lividus SINE2-1_PL: Nishihara et al. 2016 (GBE).
